Fortress

A private password manager. Your Stellar secret seed encrypts the vault. There is no account, and the data stays on your phone.

Privacy Support Delete your data

On this phone

Logins, notes, authenticator codes, addresses, cards, and passkeys are saved in an encrypted file you choose, stellar-vault.svault. Fortress does not upload that file. A backup opens on another phone with the same seed.

Nothing is sent unless you ask

Export and backup go where you send them. Send to PC copies one login to your own computer after you confirm. An optional breach check sends only the first five characters of a password hash to Have I Been Pwned, and only when you tap it.
