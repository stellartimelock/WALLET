Home Privacy Support

On this phone

  1. Open the save folder you picked in Fortress. The file is stellar-vault.svault. Older copies use the same name with a backup suffix.
  2. Delete those files if you want the encrypted vault gone. Deleting them is permanent without the seed and another copy.
  3. Uninstall Fortress. That removes the app, the fingerprint shortcut, and the autofill service. It does not always delete a folder you created in Documents.

Other copies

If you exported a backup, shared a file, or sent a login to your PC, those copies are yours. Delete them from the folder, email, or computer where you put them. We cannot delete them for you.

Breach check

If you ran a breach check, Have I Been Pwned received a short hash prefix, not your password and not your name. Fortress did not keep that request, and we have no account there to close.
