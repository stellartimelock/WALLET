Privacy Policy

At StellarTimeLock, LLC, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. Stellar TimeLock is a non-custodial wallet. Your keys stay on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not operate a backend that stores your secret seed, private keys, recovery phrase, or signing authority. The app is designed so that private keys never leave the device. We never hold user funds.

On-device storage

Wallet secrets are protected by the Android Keystore or iOS Keychain and gated behind biometric authentication where available. Android builds use allowBackup="false" to avoid accidental cloud copies of sensitive material.

Optional cloud backup

If you enable an optional encrypted backup feature in the app, only ciphertext you choose to export is uploaded to storage you control (for example your own Google Drive). The landing website never receives your seed or backup passphrase.

This website

This informational landing page does not ask for, accept, or store secret seeds, recovery phrases, or private keys. StellarTimeLock, LLC will NEVER request those secrets on this website.
