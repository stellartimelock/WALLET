Find help nearby

Free forever. No ads.

Sourceify is a free app for people who need a safe place to sleep, a meal, a clinic, benefits, an ID, a ride, or a number to call. It works across the United States, including on a cheap phone and a slow connection.

Coming soon to Google Play

The app is free. It does not sell your information.

What it is

  • Free forever Looking things up does not cost money. There is nothing to buy inside the app.
  • No ads No advertising, no tracking, and no advertising ID.
  • Privacy-first You can search with no account. Location is used only after you tap Use my location, and it is not saved as a history.
  • Nationwide Places from public lists are saved in the app by state, so a lot of it still works offline. Call before you go. Hours change.

Eight kinds of help

  • Shelter A safe place to sleep
  • Food Food banks and meals
  • Health Clinics, recovery, and crisis care
  • Benefits Programs you apply for
  • ID and mail ID, papers, and mail
  • Getting around Bus fare, rides, phone, and Wi-Fi
  • Jobs and legal Work, legal help, and refugee support
  • Emergency Emergency and crisis lines, saved on the phone

Showers, recovery, phone and Wi-Fi, refugee help, and family resources are inside those eight lists. Domestic violence shelter addresses stay hidden unless the source already made them public.

Sourceify is not an emergency service. If someone is in danger, call 911. You can do that from the help lines in the app. The app asks you to confirm before it opens the dialer.
