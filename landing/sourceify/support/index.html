Support

Write to isaacdschuster@gmail.com.

A city and the place name are enough. You do not have to send your name, your story, or where you sleep.

Suggest or correct a place

In the app:

  • Suggest a missing place is on the home screen. Add the name, what it offers, and an address.
  • Report wrong info is on a place. Pick a reason, such as wrong hours or a wrong phone. The note is optional.
  • Is this still accurate? is on a place. Say yes, or say something is wrong.

In this build those notes stay on your phone. Other people cannot see them, because no review server is configured. Email is how a correction reaches a person today. Check places nearby, on the home screen, also stays on the phone and works offline.

You do not need to sign in to look things up, to call, or to save a note on your phone.

If someone is in danger

Call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988. For domestic violence, call 1-800-799-7233. Those lines are in the app. Do not wait on an email.

The app will not open

Sourceify is coming soon to Google Play (free): package app.stellar.sourceify. It needs Android. A lot of the list works offline after the app is installed. Map pictures need a connection, and data saver keeps them off on cellular data until you tap Show map anyway.
