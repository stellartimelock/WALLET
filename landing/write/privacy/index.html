Write — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 13, 2026 · App: Write (com.stellartimelock.write) · Developer: StellarTimeLock

Write is a local documents app. It creates and stores documents on your phone.

What Write collects

Nothing. Write does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal or device data.

  • No network. Write does not send your documents or usage anywhere. It does not upload or sync to a server.
  • Local documents only. Documents you create or open stay on your phone. They are saved under StellarTimeLock backups/Write/.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up or login.
  • No ads. Write shows no advertising and contains no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking. No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

Write requests no device permissions. You choose a folder with Android's file picker; documents are saved there under StellarTimeLock backups/Write/.

Children

Write is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
