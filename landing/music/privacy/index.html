Music — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 13, 2026 · App: Music (com.stellartimelock.music) · Developer: StellarTimeLock

Music is a local music and video player. It plays files that are already on your phone.

What Music collects

Nothing. Music does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal or device data.

  • No network. Music does not send your files, library, or usage anywhere. It does not stream or download.
  • Local files only. Music reads the songs and videos on your phone (with your permission) to list and play them. Its own library file (queue, shuffle, repeat, hidden folders) is saved only on your phone.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up or login.
  • No ads. Music shows no advertising and contains no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking. No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

  • Music and audio / Videos (Android 13+), or Storage (Android 12 and older): to list and play the songs and videos on your phone.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls while music plays in the background.
  • Foreground service (media playback): so songs keep playing when the screen is off or you switch apps.
  • StellarTimeLock key picker (optional): if you use the StellarTimeLock wallet app, Music can ask it for a key to protect its local save. The key stays in memory on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Children

Music is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.
