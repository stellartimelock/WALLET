# Stellar TimeLock — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

StellarTimeLock, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Stellar TimeLock mobile application for Android (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## Information We Don't Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information for advertising or profiling. The App is designed so wallet secrets stay on your device. Specifically:

- We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers as a condition of using the wallet
- We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs for tracking
- We do not collect location data
- We do not use cookies or tracking technologies in the App for analytics
- We do not have access to your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet contents
- We do not operate cloud storage of your wallet secrets

## What Stays On Your Device

All wallet data — including your seed phrase, private keys, contacts, vault configurations, and local transaction history — is stored locally on your device under Android Keystore / SecureStore device protection, with optional biometric unlock where available. You control this data at all times. Android builds use `allowBackup="false"` to avoid accidental cloud copies of sensitive material.

## Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the following third-party services:

- **Stellar/Soroban RPC:** When you interact with vaults or the Stellar network, transactions are broadcast through RPC endpoints. These services may see your device's IP address and transaction data as necessary for network operation. We do not control these services.
- **Optional swap partners:** When you use the optional swap feature, exchange requests are sent to third-party partner APIs. Their privacy policies govern how they handle that data.

## Data Security

Your wallet data is protected by encryption derived from your Stellar secret key / device-protected key material. We cannot recover your data, reset your wallet, or access your funds.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and on the product site where applicable.
